# Technoblade Legacy

A creative tribute project honouring Technoblade through music and a documentary.

This is a personal creative project workspace. It is separate from BitBlock's core technology-video plan; it does not change BitBlock's tech-only editorial direction.

## Project components

### 1. Technoblade documentary

**Current creative direction**
- **Length:** approximately 20–30 minutes.
- **Narration:** the creator's own voice.
- **Tone:** cinematic and emotional.
- **Visual approach:** real footage of Technoblade from genuine videos and relevant source material.
- **Not the concept:** a fictional “what if his life were a movie?” treatment, an actor portraying him, or staged footage presented as real.

The documentary should tell the story through authentic footage, narration, editing, music, and carefully chosen context. Do not invent events, quotes, or scenes. Verify dates and factual claims before including them.

### 2. Tribute music

Related songs are tracked in [the Music Catalogue](../../Music/MUSIC_CATALOG.md):
- **Technoblade Tribute** — working title; celebrates his legendary gameplay, reputation, and legacy.
- **Technoblade vs Dream** — working title; based on their real $100,000 duel, which Technoblade won. The song direction includes memorable, repetitive sing-along hooks.

Keep final titles and production status updated in the central music catalogue rather than maintaining competing lists.

## Production principles

- Treat Technoblade respectfully and preserve the distinction between documented fact, narration, and interpretation.
- Use authentic footage and clearly contextualize clips; do not fabricate archival material.
- Keep narration in the creator's own voice.
- Build emotion through story structure, pacing, music, and real moments rather than invented scenes.
- Check source context, dates, and claims before finalizing the documentary.
- Consider copyright and platform rules when selecting footage, music, images, and clips. Attribution alone does not automatically grant permission.

# Content Direction — Tech-First BitBlock

## Core clarification

BitBlock should be understood as a tech-first channel.
Technology content should receive more attention and appear more often than Gaming content.
Gaming is a smaller side interest or side project within the broader channel.
The creator is not aiming for equal representation between the two content worlds.
No exact percentage, ratio, or monthly quota has been chosen.
"Side project" describes relative emphasis, not a ban on gaming uploads.
The channel can still publish gaming videos when the creator has a good idea.

## What tech-first means

When proposing video ideas, start from the creator's technology interests and current tools.
Do not force a gaming angle onto a technology topic.
Do not make the channel dependent on Minecraft for its identity.
A video about WSL, Docker Desktop, programming, or a useful workflow fits naturally.
A technology video does not need to mention ATLAS to belong on BitBlock.
Standalone topics can establish the channel before the ATLAS series begins.
A broad mix of practical and exploratory technology is preferred over one narrow tutorial niche.
The channel can show useful outcomes and the process of learning or testing.
The creator's interests and available time should guide selection.

## Balance without a quota

Avoid turning "Tech more than Gaming" into an arbitrary numeric target.
A plan may contain several Tech uploads between occasional Gaming uploads.
The spacing can change with school, project workload, and motivation.
There is no need to alternate Tech and Gaming uploads.
There is no need to publish Gaming simply because the last upload was Tech.
There is no need to delay a strong Tech idea to make room for Gaming.
A particularly compelling Gaming idea can still be published when appropriate.
Use the overall direction, not a rigid ratio, to make choices.

## Early channel identity

The initial period should establish BitBlock through regular videos made with the current setup.
The exact launch list remains undecided.
WSL and Docker Desktop were specifically mentioned as examples of current tools.
Those examples are not an exclusive list or a promise of particular uploads.
Early videos may teach, investigate, demonstrate, or combine these approaches.
The creator wants a range of content rather than one repeated format.
ATLAS is not expected to be the channel's launch content.
The channel can build an audience and recognisable style before the project series begins.

## Relationship to existing pillars

The existing Tech and Gaming pillars remain useful organisational categories.
This clarification changes their relative emphasis, not the one-channel strategy.
Do not create separate Tech and Gaming channels because of this clarification.
Do not interpret the smaller Gaming role as making Gaming irrelevant.
Keep titles, thumbnails, and playlists clear about each upload's subject.
Classify mixed topics by their main promise to the viewer.
A Minecraft server setup may be Tech-led if the focus is administration.
A modded gameplay story may be Gaming-led if the focus is the playing experience.

## Advice for the main agent

Prioritise Tech ideas when asked for a shortlist of possible next videos.
Include Gaming ideas when the creator asks or when they fit naturally.
Avoid presenting an equal split as the default recommendation.
Do not invent a target number of Tech uploads per month.
Do not treat analytics as the only reason to choose a topic.
Help the creator make videos that are useful, interesting, and sustainable.
If a plan becomes too demanding, simplify it rather than adding pressure.
Preserve the creator's freedom to change the balance over time.

Unspecified: first upload topic and precise launch sequence.

- Do not assume the creator wants to stop making Gaming videos.

## Visual production direction

Music videos and visualizers are separate production work from writing the songs. The current visual approach under consideration includes:
- A short **“For the best experience — use headphones”** opening card.
- Animated lyric typography rather than relying on large quantities of generated cinematic footage.
- Beat-synchronized transitions, motion graphics, visualizers, and instrumental sections with no lyrics on screen.
- A distinct visual identity for each musical category: dark-fantasy Minecraft, Technoblade tribute, and the mass-anthem look of **7-C: UNLEASHED**.

The first planned full music-video experiment is **7-C: UNLEASHED**, using a typography-led lyric-video approach. This is a production plan, not a completed video.

# Production Workflow

## 1. Research before lyrics

For each track:
1. Gather candidate 2026 milestones from credible primary sources and reputable reporting.
2. Record the exact date, organisation, achievement, and source.
3. Classify each item as demonstrated result, publication, announcement, release, ongoing programme, or future plan.
4. Check whether the milestone genuinely belongs in the track.
5. Remove claims that cannot be verified or are too uncertain to state confidently.

## 2. Build the song

Create a track brief before writing:
- central theme and emotional point;
- narrative arc and section plan;
- verified milestones to include;
- key people, organisations, missions, technologies, or concepts;
- track-specific musical identity;
- transition into or out of neighbouring tracks.

Lyrics should tell a coherent story, not read like a news index.

## 3. Suno generation

Working settings and constraints:
- Use **Length: Auto** in More Options.
- Aim for at least six minutes; target around 8:20 for Tracks 02–10 and around nine minutes for Track 11.
- Lyrics limit: 5,000 characters.
- Style prompt limit: 1,000 characters.
- Do not force a duration into the style prompt. Let the lyrics and musical development support the length.
- Generate versions and compare complete tracks, not only the opening.

## 4. Version management

Use clear version labels: V1, V2, V3, V4, and so on. Record:
- generation date;
- actual duration;
- lyric changes;
- style-prompt changes;
- pronunciation fixes;
- what improved;
- remaining issues;
- selected / rejected status.

Never overwrite the record of a previously selected version. When a version is final, mark it clearly in the progress tracker.

## 5. Review checklist

### Music
- Does the arrangement evolve across the full runtime?
- Are the intro, verses, chorus, bridge, climax, and outro distinct?
- Does the track have its own musical identity?
- Are the dynamics, stereo width, vocals, percussion, and bass clear?
- Does the song avoid filler and excessive repetition?

### Lyrics and facts
- Are all factual claims supported by the research log?
- Are dates and organisation names accurate?
- Are announced plans clearly distinguished from completed achievements?
- Are difficult names pronounced correctly?
- Does the recurring motif feel natural in this track?

### Album continuity
- Does the track belong in the album's narrative order?
- Does it connect musically or thematically with neighbouring tracks?
- Does the finale have room to deliver the full return of the motif?

## 6. Final album assembly

After all tracks are selected:
1. Record exact runtimes and calculate the real album duration.
2. Check the sequence and transitions.
3. Listen to the complete album in order.
4. Verify that the finale resolves the opening's promise.
5. Update the progress tracker and catalogue metadata.

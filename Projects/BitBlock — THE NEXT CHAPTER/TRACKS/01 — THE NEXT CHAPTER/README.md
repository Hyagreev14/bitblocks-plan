## Selected style prompt

Dark, deep, epic cinematic orchestral anthem with an expansive, immersive stereo soundstage. 6–8 minute progression. Powerful hybrid vocals: resonant deep male lead, ethereal female vocals and enormous layered mixed choir. Wide left-right orchestral strings, sweeping atmospheric synths, spatial whispers, rich cinematic brass and deep centred sub-bass. Thunderous taiko drums with spacious impacts and powerful stereo percussion. Begin sparse and mysterious, gradually building through evolving musical chapters. Huge emotional choruses with exceptionally wide choir harmonies, dramatic pauses and monumental orchestral drops. Clear, centred lead vocals surrounded by expansive backing layers. Tense midnight countdown, one second of complete silence, then an enormous triumphant 2027 impact. Majestic, hopeful lingering choir. Dark fantasy atmosphere, grand, emotional and futuristic yet human. No cheerful pop, party EDM or generic dance beat.

# 04 — BEYOND THE STARS

**Focus:** Astronomy, space science, and exploration  
**Working duration:** ~8:20  
**Status:** Planned

## Central idea

Humanity looks outward through telescopes, robotic explorers, observatories, and missions. The song should capture both the technical achievement and the emotional scale of exploring the cosmos.

## Research checklist

Verify 2026 milestones involving:
- space agency and commercial mission milestones;
- launches, landings, flybys, and spacecraft operations;
- telescope and observatory observations;
- planetary science and solar-system exploration;
- astronomy discoveries and published results.

Use precise mission status and dates. Do not confuse planned launches with completed events or observations with confirmed discoveries.

## Narrative arc

1. A silent sky filled with unanswered questions.
2. Instruments and missions extending human vision.
3. Specific verified discoveries and exploration milestones.
4. Earth seen as one shared home.
5. A soaring cosmic climax.

## Musical direction

Expansive space-ambient textures, soaring strings, distant choir, slow taiko impacts, and enormous brass. Use a spacious opening and a wide, emotionally uplifting final chorus.

## Required elements

- Keep the album motif intact while adapting the surrounding imagery to space.
- Balance wonder with factual precision.

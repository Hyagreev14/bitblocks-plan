# 05 — WORLDS WE ARE BUILDING

**Focus:** Computing, semiconductors, electronics, and digital infrastructure  
**Working duration:** ~8:20  
**Status:** Planned

## Central idea

Modern life is built on layers of engineering: transistors, chips, memory, networks, software, data centres, and the people who design them. The song should reveal the hidden foundations beneath the digital world.

## Research checklist

Verify 2026 milestones in:
- semiconductor fabrication, packaging, and chip architecture;
- processors, accelerators, memory, and computing systems;
- networking, connectivity, and digital infrastructure;
- software systems and developer tools where a major milestone is justified;
- energy efficiency and practical limits of computing.

Separate product announcements from shipping products and independently measured performance.

## Narrative arc

1. A pulse inside a silent machine.
2. Silicon, circuits, and the architecture of computation.
3. Systems connecting devices, people, and knowledge.
4. The scale and cost of building the digital future.
5. A powerful industrial-orchestral climax.

## Musical direction

Mechanical rhythmic motifs and crisp electronic detail layered into a dark orchestral framework. Use rhythmic precision without turning the song into generic EDM.

## Required elements

- Make technical ideas understandable through vivid but accurate metaphors.
- Return to the recurring album refrain.

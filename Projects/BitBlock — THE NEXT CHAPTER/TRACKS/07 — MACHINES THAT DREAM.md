# 07 — MACHINES THAT DREAM

**Focus:** Robotics, automation, and embodied intelligence  
**Working duration:** ~8:20  
**Status:** Planned

## Central idea

Intelligence is moving beyond screens into machines that sense, move, manipulate objects, and work in physical environments. The song should explore the meeting point of mechanics, software, and human purpose.

## Research checklist

Verify 2026 developments in:
- humanoid and mobile robotics;
- industrial, medical, agricultural, and service robots;
- embodied AI and robot learning;
- real-world deployments and demonstrated capabilities;
- safety, reliability, and human-robot collaboration.

Distinguish a controlled demonstration from reliable operation in everyday environments.

## Narrative arc

1. The first mechanical movement in the dark.
2. Sensors, motion, balance, and learning.
3. Robots entering useful environments.
4. The role of people, design, and responsibility.
5. A kinetic, powerful climax.

## Musical direction

Kinetic percussion, mechanical rhythmic motifs, synthetic textures, and a strong orchestral backbone. Contrast precise rhythmic passages with broad human choir sections.

## Required elements

- Avoid portraying machines as magical or independently conscious.
- Focus on demonstrated engineering and human collaboration.
- Bring back the album motif.

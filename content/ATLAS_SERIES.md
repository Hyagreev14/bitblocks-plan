# Building ATLAS — Series Plan

## Concept
A recurring BitBlock Tech series documenting the development of ATLAS session by session. Each episode covers a real work session or a coherent milestone, using a screenshot-based timelapse and a full voiceover.

The series should show the evolution of the project: planning, design decisions, implementation, debugging, revisions, and milestones. It is both a project diary and an understandable explanation of the work.

## Capture method
The creator does not plan to record continuous video of the desktop. The intended method is a Chronolapse-style tool that captures a screenshot periodically—initially around every 5 or 10 seconds—then assembles the images into a timelapse. The interval can change after testing.

## Narration
Every episode should have a voiceover explaining the session. It should not merely list clicks or keystrokes. Explain:
- the goal for the session;
- relevant context from earlier work;
- what was changed and why;
- important decisions and trade-offs;
- bugs or obstacles and how they were approached;
- what works now, what remains incomplete, and what comes next.

## Episode types
- Planning: product direction, feature scope, architecture, UI concepts, protocol design, and trade-offs.
- Development: implementation shown through the timelapse and narrated explanation.
- Investigation: debugging, testing, performance, or compatibility questions.
- Milestone: a meaningful working result or release checkpoint.
- Retrospective: what changed, what was learned, and what should be redesigned.

## Continuity
Use a consistent series name and episode numbering only when it helps viewers follow the journey. Each video should still make sense to a new viewer. Briefly recap only the context needed.

## Honesty
Do not imply that ATLAS is finished or that a feature works unless it has been tested. Show prototypes as prototypes and plans as plans. It is fine to say a design changed.

## Protect the project
Review captures before publication. Keep credentials, tokens, private repositories, personal files, and private notifications out of the video. See the privacy guide.

## Flexible scope
Not every work session needs an episode. Combine tiny sessions when they form one story, and split a very large session if it contains distinct viewer-friendly topics. The project should not be slowed down just to produce content.

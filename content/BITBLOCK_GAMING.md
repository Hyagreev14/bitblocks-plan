# BitBlock Gaming

## Scope
Gaming content includes Minecraft and other games. Minecraft may be a recurring focus, but it is not an exclusive rule.

## Topic areas
- Minecraft experiments and discoveries.
- Mods, modpacks, and mod development.
- Gameplay systems, mechanics, challenges, and creative builds.
- World-generation or technical curiosities.
- Other games when there is a compelling premise.

## Video approaches
- A clear challenge or experiment.
- A discovery explained through gameplay.
- A development or testing diary for a game-related project.
- A comparison that uses fair and transparent criteria.
- A story-led walkthrough with a meaningful outcome.

## Presentation
Make the premise clear early. Use gameplay footage, diagrams, labels, or narration to explain what is happening. Avoid padding a video with repetitive footage.

## Technical accuracy
When discussing game versions, mods, loaders, performance, or compatibility, verify the exact versions used. Distinguish personal experience from general compatibility claims.

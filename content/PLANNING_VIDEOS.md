# Planning Videos

## Why publish planning videos?
Planning videos explain the reasoning behind a project. Development timelapses show what changed; planning videos explain why the project is being shaped that way. Together they create a fuller development story.

## Suitable subjects
- Introducing the project vision and intended users.
- Defining the first release and separating Core from Pro.
- Designing the architecture and major components.
- Exploring UI layouts and navigation.
- Designing the ATLAS Action Protocol (AAP).
- Comparing possible approaches and explaining trade-offs.
- Reviewing a feature idea and deciding whether it belongs.
- Revisiting a previous decision after learning something new.

## Visual material
A planning video can use diagrams, sketches, mockups, a notes document, a feature map, a code-free screen walkthrough, or a narrated slide sequence. It does not require face-camera footage.

## Suggested structure
1. **Context:** what problem or decision is being considered?
2. **Constraints:** what must the solution support or avoid?
3. **Options:** what approaches are plausible?
4. **Reasoning:** what are the advantages, disadvantages, and unknowns?
5. **Decision:** what is being chosen now, and what remains provisional?
6. **Next step:** how will the plan be tested or implemented?

## Keep it authentic
Show the actual thought process, including ideas that were rejected or changed. Do not present uncertain plans as completed engineering. Avoid turning every small decision into a full video; choose decisions with enough context or viewer value.

## Relationship to development episodes
Planning and development belong to the same Building ATLAS journey. They can be separate episodes, combined when the story is clearer, or published in whichever order best explains the project.

# Video Formats

BitBlock is technology-focused. Choose the format that serves the technical idea; do not force every video into a template.

## Beginner tutorial
A focused explanation that helps viewers complete a task or understand a workflow. Cover prerequisites, tested environment, steps, expected result, and limitations. Explain assumptions rather than expecting prior knowledge. Avoid padding and distinguish a personal preference from the only correct approach.

## Explainer
A concept-led video that makes a system or idea understandable. Use diagrams, examples, and demonstrations rather than relying on definitions alone.

## Documented experiment
Begin with a question, curiosity, or hypothesis worth testing. Explain the setup and method, show meaningful stages, and report the actual result—including surprises, failure, and limitations. Do not rewrite an unsuccessful experiment as a success story or claim more than the test demonstrates.

## Interesting workflow
Show a useful or interesting way to accomplish something by combining tools, settings, scripts, or repeatable steps. Explain the problem first, demonstrate the outcome, and mention trade-offs or situations where the workflow may not fit. Do not claim that a personal workflow is universally optimal.

## Development diary or timelapse
Document real progress on a software or technical project. A screenshot sequence can provide the visual record while narration explains the purpose, decisions, challenges, and outcome. For the Building ATLAS series, use the dedicated screenshot-timelapse and voiceover workflow.

## Planning/design episode
Explore a real design problem through diagrams, notes, mockups, specifications, comparisons, and narration. Make clear what is an idea, a decision, a prototype, or an implemented feature.

## Review or comparison
Use explicit criteria and real experience. State what was tested, what was not tested, and who may find the result useful.

## Technical demonstration using game footage
Game footage may be used to demonstrate a technical result, such as a Minecraft mod feature or a test environment. Keep the focus on development, configuration, debugging, compatibility, or technical behaviour. Do not turn it into gameplay commentary, a Let's Play, or a gaming-focused video.

## Format selection
Tutorials, experiments, and workflows can overlap. Choose the main approach based on why a viewer would watch, and use supporting formats only when they improve clarity.

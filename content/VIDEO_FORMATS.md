# Video Formats

## Tutorial
A focused explanation that helps viewers complete a task or understand a workflow. Include prerequisites, tested environment, steps, expected result, and limitations.

## Explainer
A concept-led video that makes a system or idea understandable. Use diagrams and examples rather than relying on definitions alone.

## Experiment
A question is tested with a clear method. Explain what is being compared, what is controlled, what happened, and what cannot be concluded.

## Development timelapse
A screenshot sequence provides visual evidence of work over time. Narration supplies context and meaning. Use zooms or cutaways when important changes are too small to see.

## Planning/design episode
A decision or design problem is explored through diagrams, notes, mockups, and voiceover.

## Gaming walkthrough or challenge
A clear premise drives the video. Show meaningful progress and explain rules or mechanics when needed.

## Review or comparison
Use explicit criteria and real experience. State what was tested, what was not tested, and who may find the result useful.

## Format selection
Choose the format that serves the idea. Do not force every video into a template or make a format more complicated than the subject needs.

# Audience

## Intended viewers
BitBlock is for curious viewers who enjoy technology, making things, and understanding how systems work. The audience may include beginner developers, students, hobbyist programmers, Linux and self-hosting enthusiasts, and people who like practical experiments and software projects.

Do not assume every viewer is an expert. Explain unfamiliar terms when they matter, but do not talk down to viewers who already know the basics.

## Viewer needs
Different videos serve different needs:
- **Learn:** understand a tool, concept, workflow, or technical decision.
- **Build:** see a project take shape and learn from its implementation.
- **Explore:** discover an interesting experiment or unexpected result.
- **Apply:** follow a practical method or workflow to solve a problem.
- **Relate:** see honest problem-solving rather than a polished story that hides every setback.

A technical demonstration may use game footage when it helps explain a mod or technical result, but the audience promise remains about the technology—not gameplay.

## Accessibility
- Explain acronyms on first use when the context requires it.
- Put commands and important settings on screen long enough to read.
- Use captions where practical and check automatic captions for technical terms.
- Avoid relying only on colour to communicate important information.
- Keep narration understandable; music should not compete with speech.

## Viewer trust
The channel should clearly distinguish tested results from expectations, personal preference, and speculation. If a method has limitations, say so. If a video is a work-in-progress update, label it as such.

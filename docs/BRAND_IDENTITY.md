# Brand Identity

## Name
**BitBlock** is the channel name. Keep spelling and capitalisation consistent.

## Tagline
**CODE. CREATE. EXPLORE.**

It communicates curiosity, software development, and making things with technology. Use it where it fits; it does not need to be attached to every title or graphic.

## Visual direction
Current working direction:
- dark charcoal, graphite, or near-black foundations;
- electric cyan or icy blue as a primary accent;
- restrained green as a secondary signal;
- clean geometric shapes that can suggest pixels, blocks, circuits, or connected bits;
- modern developer and technology aesthetic without visual clutter.

This is a direction, not a locked colour specification. Test legibility at small sizes before finalising.

## Logo and avatar
Aim for a compact emblem that remains recognisable as a small circular avatar. A geometric block/bit motif can suggest code, digital systems, and connected components. Avoid tiny text and overly detailed illustrations.

## Banner
The banner should make the channel name and technology/software-development focus obvious. Keep important elements within the platform's safe area and preview on desktop, mobile, and television layouts.

## Thumbnail language
- One clear subject or visual question.
- Strong contrast and readable composition at small size.
- Minimal text; use text only when it adds context.
- Consistent visual quality, not identical layouts.
- Do not use misleading screenshots or imply results the video does not show.

## Naming system
Use BitBlock as the channel identity. Use descriptive series labels such as **Building ATLAS** for content organisation, not as separate channel identities unless a future change is deliberately approved.

# Channel Architecture

## Current structure
One YouTube channel: **BitBlock**, focused on technology and software development.

Organise uploads with:
- playlists for technical topics or recurring projects;
- named playlists for series such as Building ATLAS;
- consistent series labels in titles or descriptions where helpful;
- channel sections that make the technical subjects easy to browse.

There is no separate Gaming pillar or Gaming channel in the current plan.

## Editorial boundary for game footage
BitBlock does not publish gameplay-focused videos. Game footage may appear only when it directly demonstrates a technical subject—for example, showing a Minecraft mod feature during a video about its development. The main viewer promise must remain technical.

## Why one channel
A single BitBlock channel keeps the identity and publishing workflow simple. The channel can cover a broad range of technology without splitting its audience or creating unnecessary administration.

## Naming and consistency
Do not use BitBlock Gaming as a current sub-brand or playlist. The main channel remains BitBlock. Organise videos by technical subject and recurring project when useful.

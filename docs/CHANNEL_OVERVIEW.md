# Channel Overview

## What BitBlock is
BitBlock is a faceless, creator-led **technology and software-development channel**. It follows curiosity through learning, experimenting, designing, building, and explaining real things. It should feel like a person sharing useful discoveries and genuine project work—not a content farm or corporate tutorial library.

BitBlock is tech-only in its editorial purpose. It does not publish gameplay-focused videos, Let's Plays, Survival or Hardcore episodes, gaming challenges, or gaming series. Footage from a game is allowed when it is needed to demonstrate a technical subject—for example, showing a Minecraft mod's feature while explaining its development. In those videos, the subject remains the technology or development, not playing the game.

## Technology covered
BitBlock is intentionally broad. Subjects may include:
- Programming, software development, debugging, and developer workflows.
- Linux, WSL, terminals, command-line tools, and system administration.
- Docker, containers, servers, self-hosting, VPS concepts, and networking.
- AI tools, APIs, coding assistants, local models, and automation.
- Windows developer tools, useful utilities, and productivity workflows.
- Hardware, homelabs, peripherals, and practical computing.
- Defensive cybersecurity education, privacy, and safe technical labs.
- Original projects such as ATLAS and technical projects such as Minecraft mod development.

Technology content does not need a Minecraft connection or any connection to ATLAS.

## The promise to viewers
A viewer should leave with at least one of these:
- a clearer understanding of a technology or idea;
- a practical method they can try safely;
- a useful view into how a real project is designed and built;
- an interesting experiment or discovery;
- a sense of the decisions, trade-offs, and lessons behind the result.

## Creator-led identity
The channel is faceless, not anonymous in personality. Voiceover, opinions, choices, mistakes, experiments, and explanations create the point of view. The creator does not need to appear on camera.

## Current positioning
A broad technology-and-development channel with a practical maker perspective. It can grow into recognisable recurring series while remaining flexible enough for different technical interests.

## What this is not
- Not a gaming or gameplay channel.
- Not exclusively a Minecraft channel.
- Not exclusively a Linux or coding tutorial channel.
- Not a promise to upload on a strict schedule regardless of school or project workload.
- Not a requirement to turn every hobby or project into content.
- Not a claim that every experiment will succeed.

## Working tagline
**CODE. CREATE. EXPLORE.**

This tagline is a useful brand direction, not a rule that must appear in every asset.

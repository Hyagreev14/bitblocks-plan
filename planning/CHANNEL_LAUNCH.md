# Channel Launch Plan

## Goal
Launch BitBlock with a clear identity and enough prepared ideas to avoid starting from a blank page. The launch should be simple and achievable.

## Before the first upload
- Finalise a usable profile image and banner.
- Write a channel description that reflects both Tech and Gaming.
- Set up basic channel sections and playlists when content exists.
- Choose an initial video idea that can be completed with available tools and time.
- Test voice recording and editing with a short sample.
- Check that captures and assets do not expose private information.

## First content mix
There is no required ratio. A sensible start is to publish the strongest ready video from either pillar, then continue with the topics the creator genuinely wants to make. Avoid forcing a Gaming upload just to balance a Tech upload, or vice versa.

## Launch video options
Potential introductions include:
- a short channel introduction explaining what BitBlock covers;
- a strong standalone Tech or Gaming video that demonstrates the channel's style;
- an ATLAS introduction if the project is ready to be presented clearly.

A channel introduction is optional. A useful video can introduce the channel better than a generic announcement.

## After launch
Focus on making the next video. Use early feedback as information, not as a verdict. Adjust packaging and pacing gradually.

# Episode Planning

## One-page episode brief
For each substantial video, record:
- **Working title:** a clear description of the topic.
- **Pillar:** Tech, Gaming, or overlap.
- **Format:** tutorial, explainer, experiment, planning, timelapse, walkthrough, or other.
- **Viewer promise:** what will the viewer understand or experience?
- **Premise:** what question, problem, or story drives the video?
- **Environment:** versions, hardware, software, or game context where relevant.
- **Evidence:** what must be tested, captured, or researched?
- **Visuals:** screen captures, timelapse, diagrams, gameplay, or mockups.
- **Narration:** full script or outline.
- **Risks:** privacy, technical uncertainty, scope, or production constraints.
- **Ending:** result, limitations, and natural next step.

## Scope
An episode should have one main story. If the outline contains several unrelated topics, split it or narrow the focus. If the idea is too small, combine it with a related update rather than padding it.

## ATLAS-specific planning
For a Building ATLAS episode, identify the session or milestone, list the meaningful changes, note any decisions worth explaining, and confirm that the capture sequence is usable. The voiceover should explain the work, not merely describe the screen.

## Status labels
Use simple labels such as idea, researching, planned, captured, editing, ready, published. Do not mark a video as captured or published until it actually is.

# Review and Iteration

## Purpose
Improve the next video through a small, repeatable review. Do not turn analytics into a daily distraction.

## Review timing
Review once there is enough data to be meaningful. Early results may be volatile, especially on a new channel. There is no universal number of hours or views that guarantees a reliable conclusion.

## Review dimensions
1. **Idea:** did the topic solve a real question or create genuine interest?
2. **Packaging:** did title and thumbnail set an accurate expectation?
3. **Opening:** did the first section explain why the viewer should continue?
4. **Clarity:** were explanations and on-screen text understandable?
5. **Pacing:** were there slow or repetitive sections?
6. **Delivery:** was narration clear and audio balanced?
7. **Audience response:** what did comments and retention suggest?
8. **Effort:** was the workflow sustainable?

## Make small changes
Choose one or two improvements for the next upload. Avoid changing topic, title style, editing, length, and format all at once unless there is a clear reason.

## Keep a learning log
Record the date, video, observations, and next experiment. Distinguish measured evidence from personal impressions. Do not treat one upload as proof that an entire content pillar will or will not work.

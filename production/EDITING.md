# Editing Guide

## Editing objective
Make the video easy to follow, not excessively flashy. Every cut, zoom, label, sound, or graphic should help the viewer understand or stay oriented.

## Visual hierarchy
- The main work should remain the focus.
- Use zooms or crops to make important code and UI changes visible.
- Use short chapter labels to signal major sections.
- Use diagrams when the screen capture cannot explain an architecture or concept.
- Keep text large enough to read on a normal phone screen.

## Timelapse pacing
A timelapse can move quickly through repetitive work, but slow down or insert a close-up when an important change occurs. Do not make every frame equally fast if that makes the work impossible to understand.

## Audio
Prioritise clear narration. Music should support the mood and remain quieter than speech. Avoid abrupt changes in loudness. Check the video on speakers and headphones if possible.

## Captions and labels
Correct important technical terms in captions. Verify commands, file names, version numbers, and labels. Do not rely on auto-captions without review.

## Export review
Watch the complete exported file. Check the start and end, visual glitches, missing media, audio sync, text legibility, aspect ratio, and privacy. Export settings should be selected based on the editor and platform's current recommendations rather than copied blindly from an old preset.

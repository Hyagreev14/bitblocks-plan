# Privacy and Safety for Production

## Before capture
- Close password managers, authentication prompts, private chats, email, and unrelated personal documents.
- Keep API keys, tokens, passwords, recovery codes, SSH private keys, and session cookies off-screen.
- Avoid exposing personal addresses, phone numbers, school details, account identifiers, or other private information.
- Use test accounts and non-sensitive sample data when demonstrating systems.

## During capture
A screenshot tool may capture unexpected pop-ups or notifications. Use focus/do-not-disturb settings where appropriate and keep the capture region limited to the intended workspace.

## After capture
Review the full image sequence, not only the final timelapse. Remove frames that reveal sensitive information. Check diagrams, terminal history, browser tabs, repository names, and filenames for accidental disclosure.

## Technical safety
Demonstrations should be defensive, educational, and conducted on systems the creator owns or is authorised to use. Do not expose real targets or credentials. Explain potentially disruptive commands before showing them and use safe test environments.

## AI and generated material
Check generated diagrams, images, scripts, and explanations for accuracy and accidental inclusion of private information. Do not imply generated visuals are real screenshots or test results.

## Final check
If uncertain whether information should be public, leave it out. A video does not need private details to be useful.

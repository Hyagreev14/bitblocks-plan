# Publishing Checklist

## Title
- State the subject or viewer benefit clearly.
- Use specific wording rather than vague hype.
- Keep the promise accurate to the actual video.
- Avoid implying a result, benchmark, or feature that was not achieved.

## Thumbnail
- Make the main subject obvious at small size.
- Use strong contrast and a simple visual hierarchy.
- Keep text brief and readable.
- Match the video rather than exaggerating it.
- Preview at small size before upload.

## Description
Include a concise summary, relevant context, chapters when useful, and links that viewers genuinely need. Mention versions or test environment for technical videos when relevant. Credit external assets where required.

## Upload settings
Choose the correct playlist, language, audience settings, visibility, and captions. Review platform settings rather than assuming previous defaults still apply.

## Final review
Watch the export and check title, thumbnail, description, captions, links, playlist, privacy, and technical claims. Confirm that no private information appears in the video.

## After publishing
Check that the public video plays correctly and that links work. Do not immediately change everything based on a tiny sample of analytics.

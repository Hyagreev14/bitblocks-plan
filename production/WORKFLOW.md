# Production Workflow

## 1. Select the idea
Write one sentence describing the viewer promise. Identify whether the video is Tech, Gaming, or an overlap. Decide whether it is a tutorial, explainer, experiment, development episode, planning episode, or another format.

## 2. Check feasibility
Estimate the work needed for research, capture, narration, and editing. Confirm that the topic is worth the time. Reduce scope rather than forcing an unrealistic schedule.

## 3. Research and test
For technical topics, verify current versions and commands. Test the exact workflow where possible. Record the environment, assumptions, limitations, and sources.

## 4. Prepare visuals
Collect screenshots, diagrams, gameplay, timelapse images, mockups, or other material. Check that the visuals actually support the narration.

## 5. Build a narration outline
Use a beginning, a clear middle, and a result. Write a full script when accuracy or structure needs it; use bullet points when a natural explanation is better.

## 6. Record voiceover
Record in a quiet environment. Do a short test first. Keep a natural pace and re-record unclear sections rather than trying to repair everything in editing.

## 7. Edit
Remove dead time, add readable labels and zooms, balance narration and music, and ensure the final video makes sense without hidden context.

## 8. Quality check
Review the complete export. Check audio, captions, spelling, links, privacy, technical accuracy, and title/thumbnail honesty.

## 9. Publish
Write a clear title and description, choose a relevant thumbnail, add the correct playlist, and publish when ready.

## 10. Learn
Review useful audience feedback and analytics after publication. Record one or two improvements for the next video.

This is a flexible checklist, not a mandatory bureaucracy. Skip steps that genuinely do not apply.
